Reading Japanese and stuck on the grammar? Point your camera at the page and Grammar Scan highlights every grammar point it finds.

SCAN ANYTHING
• Live camera: grammar points are highlighted in real time, color-coded by JLPT level
• Capture a page for a detailed analysis — every grammar point is highlighted in the image and the text
• Works with screenshots and photos from your library too
• Overlapping grammar? Choose which one to read

CLEAR EXPLANATIONS
For every grammar point:
• Meaning — each sense explained
• Usage — formation, conjugation and pitfalls
• When to use — spoken vs. written, polite vs. casual
• Origin — where the grammar comes from
• Examples — Japanese with translations, grammar highlighted
• Similar grammar — clear comparisons, one tap away

LIBRARY & FAVORITES
• Built-in JLPT N5–N2 grammar, browse by level or search
• Save the grammar you want to review

PRIVATE & OFFLINE
• Text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone — no images are uploaded
• No account, no internet, no ads, no tracking

Explanations available in English, 한국어, Español, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文 and 繁體中文 — the app follows your device language.

- **關鍵字**：日語,日文,語法,文法,JLPT,日檢,N1,N2,N3,N4,N5,拍照,掃描,OCR,學日文
- **描述**：

讀日文書時遇到看不懂的語法？打開「語法掃描」，對準書頁即可。

【拍照即辨識】
• 即時取景：畫面中的語法點即時標示，顏色依 JLPT 等級區分

• 相簿辨識：截圖或照片同樣可以辨識
• 重疊的語法點可自行選擇

【講解詳盡】
• 意思 · 用法（接續）· 使用場景 · 詞源 · 例句 · 相近語法比較

【語法庫與收藏】
• 內建 N5–N2 常用語法，可依等級瀏覽、搜尋
