## English (U.S.) / English (U.K.)

- **Name**: Japanese Grammar Lookup
- **Subtitle**: JLPT N5-N1 grammar by photo
- **Promotional Text**: Point your camera at any Japanese book and every grammar point lights up. Tap to see meaning, usage, origin and similar grammar, then review at the right time. Free.
- **Keywords**: nihongo,bunpo,learn,study,reading,textbook,camera,scanner,ocr,n4,n3,n2,exam,dictionary
- **Description**:

Reading Japanese and stuck on the grammar? Point your camera at the page and Japanese Grammar Lookup highlights every grammar point it finds.

SCAN ANYTHING
• Live camera: grammar points are highlighted in real time, color-coded by JLPT level
• Capture a page for a detailed analysis — every grammar point is highlighted in the image and the text
• Works with screenshots and photos from your library too
• Overlapping grammar? Choose which one to read

CLEAR EXPLANATIONS
For every grammar point:
• Meaning — each sense explained
• Usage — formation, conjugation and pitfalls
• When to use — spoken vs. written, polite vs. casual
• Origin — where the grammar comes from
• Examples — Japanese with translations, grammar highlighted
• Similar grammar — clear comparisons, one tap away

LIBRARY & FAVORITES
• 878 JLPT N5–N1 grammar points built in — browse by level or search
• Save the grammar you want to keep

REVIEW AT THE RIGHT TIME
• Grammar you look up is scheduled for review before you forget it
• When it's time, the home screen shows how many are ready — tap, read, and rate yourself

PRIVATE
• Text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone — no images are uploaded; scanning and explanations work offline
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Only anonymous usage statistics and feedback you choose to send are collected, never linked to you

Completely free — no in-app purchases.

Explanations available in English, 한국어, Español, Tiếng Việt, မြန်မာ, 简体中文 and 繁體中文 — the app follows your device language.

- **副標題**：學日文 JLPT N1-N5 文法拍照辨識
- **宣傳文字**：把相機對準日語書，文中的語法點立刻反白標示！點一下就能看到意思、用法、使用場景、詞源和相近語法比較；看過的語法到時間提醒你複習。全部免費。
- **關鍵字**：N2,N3,N4,日檢,語法,能力考,日本語,五十音,掃描,OCR,讀書,考試,自學,備考,字典,入門
- **描述**：

讀日文書時遇到看不懂的語法？打開「日語文法速查」，對準書頁即可。

【拍照即辨識】
• 即時取景：畫面中的語法點即時標示，顏色依 JLPT 等級區分
• 定格精讀：所有語法點在圖片與文字中全部標示
• 相簿辨識：截圖或照片同樣可以辨識
• 重疊的語法點可自行選擇

【講解詳盡】
• 意思 · 用法（接續）· 使用場景 · 詞源 · 例句 · 相近語法比較

【語法庫與收藏】
• 內建 N5–N1 共 878 個語法點，可依等級瀏覽、搜尋
• 收藏重點語法，隨時翻看

【到時間就複習】
• 查過的語法依遺忘規律排好複習時間
• 首頁提示「N 個語法可以複習了」，點一下看講解、自評記住了沒有

【隱私】
• 文字辨識在手機本機完成，不上傳任何圖片；沒網路也能掃描和看講解
• 不需註冊，無廣告、無追蹤
• 只收集匿名使用統計和你主動送出的回饋，不與身分關聯

完全免費，無 App 內購買。
